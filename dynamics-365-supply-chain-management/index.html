Overview

What Supply Chain Management is

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management is Microsoft’s ERP for larger and more complex operations. It runs procurement, inventory, warehousing, manufacturing, planning and transportation, and is usually implemented with Dynamics 365 Finance.

It is built for scale: many warehouses and plants, several legal entities, high order volumes and detailed control over how work happens on the floor.

  • Warehouse management. Waves, work, license plates and a mobile app for every warehouse task.
  • Manufacturing. Discrete, process and lean production in one system.
  • Planning. Demand forecasts and master planning across sites.
  • Transportation. Load planning, carrier rating and freight reconciliation.
  • Procurement and inventory. Vendor collaboration, quality control, batch and serial tracking.

Business Central or Supply Chain Management?

Both are Microsoft ERPs, built for different sizes of business. Because we work with both, we can give you a straight answer about which one fits.

Our approach

How we run an implementation

Senior consultants from the first workshop to the last day of support, with no hand-off to a junior team.

  • Discovery. Workshops on site, walking your warehouses and plants to see how work really happens.
  • Solution design. A written design of each process, agreed before configuration starts.
  • Build in phases. Configuration in short cycles, reviewed with your team as it takes shape.
  • Testing on real scenarios. End-to-end tests with your own orders, items and devices.

  • Go-live and support. On-site help at go-live, then support from the same consultants.
FAQ

Supply Chain Management questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

Do you implement Supply Chain Management from scratch?

Yes. We run new implementations end to end, from discovery and solution design through configuration, data migration, testing, training, go-live and support afterwards.

Is Supply Chain Management the same as Finance and Operations?

Supply Chain Management is one of Microsoft’s Dynamics 365 finance and operations apps. It is usually implemented together with Dynamics 365 Finance, which covers the general ledger, payables, receivables and financial reporting.

How do we know if we need Supply Chain Management rather than Business Central?

Warehouse volume and complexity, the number of sites and legal entities, manufacturing type and transportation needs are the main signals. We know both products and will tell you honestly which fits, including when Business Central is enough. About Business Central →

Can you move us from Dynamics AX 2012?

Yes. Microsoft provides an upgrade path from AX 2012 to Dynamics 365 finance and operations apps. We assess your customizations and data first, so the move removes what you no longer need instead of carrying it forward.

How much does an implementation cost?

Every Supply Chain Management project is quoted after discovery, based on sites, processes, integrations and data. We give you a written, fixed scope before work starts.
