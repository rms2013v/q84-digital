Dynamics 365 warehouse management

Warehouse management in Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management for high-volume, multi-site and automated warehouses. We implement it from scratch, with or without the rest of Supply Chain Management.

Overview

A warehouse system built into the ERP

Warehouse management in Supply Chain Management controls every movement in the warehouse as work: what to do, where, in what order and by whom. Workers follow that work step by step on the Warehouse Management mobile app.

Because it is part of the ERP, orders, inventory, transportation and finance see the same picture as the warehouse floor, in real time.

  • Directed work. The system tells each worker what to pick, put away or move next.
  • Rules, not memory. Put-away, picking and replenishment follow rules you configure.
  • Pallet-level control. License plates track every pallet and carton.
  • Scales across sites. One setup, rolled out warehouse by warehouse.
Capabilities

What it covers

Inbound

Receiving against purchase orders, advance ship notices and license plates, with quality checks and directed put-away.

Location directives

Rules that decide where goods are stored and picked from, by item, zone, quantity and unit.

Waves and work

Orders released in waves, with work templates that split picking into the steps your process needs.

Picking strategies

Order, batch, cluster and zone picking, with work pools so the right people get the right work.

Packing and containers

Packing stations, containerization and shipping labels, so cartons are built and labelled the same way every time.

Replenishment

Min-max, wave-demand and load-demand replenishment keeps pick faces stocked before pickers arrive.

Cycle counting

Counting plans and thresholds, so inventory stays accurate without shutting the warehouse.

Tracking

Batch and serial numbers, expiry dates with first-expired-first-out picking, and catch weight items.

Loads and shipping

Loads, shipments and outbound docks, linked to transportation management for carriers and rates.

WMS only

Keep your ERP, upgrade your warehouse

Supply Chain Management can run in warehouse management only mode. Your current ERP keeps orders, purchasing and finance; Supply Chain Management runs the warehouse.

It suits companies whose ERP works for the business but not for the warehouse floor, and who want an enterprise warehouse system without replacing everything at once.

  • Orders flow in. Your ERP sends inbound and outbound shipment orders.
  • The warehouse runs in Supply Chain Management. Receiving, put-away, picking, packing and shipping, on the mobile app.
  • Confirmations flow back. Receipts and shipments are reported to your ERP for invoicing and accounting.
  • A path forward. Move more of the business to Supply Chain Management later, if and when it makes sense.
BC or SCM

When Business Central warehousing is not enough

Business Central handles bins, directed put-away and pick well, and scanning apps add a lot. These are the signs a warehouse has outgrown it.

  • Volume. Thousands of order lines a day, with picking that has to be planned in waves.
  • Several warehouses that need the same rules, run the same way.
  • Automation. Conveyors, sorters, shuttles or robots that need tasks from the ERP.
  • Complex handling. Mixed pallets, containerization, catch weight or strict expiry rules.
  • Transportation. Loads, carriers and dock appointments planned together with the warehouse.
Our approach

How we roll out warehouse management

Warehouses go live on the floor, not in a meeting room. Our consultants are there for it.

  • Floor walk. We map how goods are received, stored, picked, packed and shipped today, and what has to change.
  • Process design. Location directives, work templates, waves and replenishment designed and agreed with your team.
  • Labels and devices. Barcodes, license plate and shipping labels, printers and handhelds tested on your Wi-Fi.
  • Testing with real volume. Full days of orders run through the system before go-live.
  • Training on the floor. Short, hands-on sessions for each role, on the devices they will use.
  • Go-live and rollout. On-site support at the first site, then the template rolled out to the rest.
FAQ

Warehouse management questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

Can we use Dynamics 365 warehouse management without replacing our ERP?

Yes. Supply Chain Management can run in warehouse management only mode, where it manages the warehouse while your existing ERP keeps orders and finance. The two systems exchange inbound and outbound shipment orders and receipt and shipment confirmations.

What devices does the mobile app run on?

The Warehouse Management mobile app runs on Android, iOS and Windows devices. Most warehouses use rugged handhelds with built-in barcode scanners, plus vehicle-mounted devices for forklifts.

Can it work with conveyors, sorters or robots?

Yes. Supply Chain Management can exchange tasks with material handling equipment and warehouse automation, either through Microsoft’s material handling equipment interface or a custom integration.

Does it handle batch, serial and expiry tracking?

Yes, including batch and serial numbers, expiry dates with first-expired-first-out picking, and catch weight items sold by weight.

Should we roll out all warehouses at once?

Usually not. We set up one site as the template, go live there, and roll out the remaining sites in waves using what the first site taught us.

Is Business Central’s warehousing enough for us?

For many small and midsize distributors it is, especially with a scanning app. Supply Chain Management makes sense when volume, the number of sites or the complexity of your processes outgrows it. Business Central scanning app →
