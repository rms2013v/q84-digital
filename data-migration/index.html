Worked example · Illustrative scenario

Moving a 25-person distributor off QuickBooks Desktop

A distributor runs accounting in QuickBooks Desktop with around 1,800 customers. Its 4,000 items and stock levels for two warehouses live in spreadsheets, and month-end takes a week of reconciling.

StageWhat happens
Weeks 1 to 2Map QuickBooks customers, vendors and accounts to Business Central. Merge the item spreadsheets into one list and agree cleansing rules with the owner.
Week 3First trial load into a test company. Duplicate customers and inconsistent unit-of-measure codes are found and fixed.
Week 4Finance posts test invoices and payments. The warehouse lead checks stock by location and bin.
Week 5Second trial load. Receivables, payables and the trial balance reconcile to QuickBooks.
Cutover weekendPhysical stock count, final load of open items and opening balances, and sign-off before Monday trading.

Outcome: The company starts in Business Central with balances that match QuickBooks to the cent, one item list and stock it can trust, with no gap in trading.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

How long does a data migration take?

For most small and midsize businesses the migration runs alongside the implementation over three to six weeks, with at least two trial loads before the final cutover. The final load itself usually happens over a weekend or at a month-end.

Do we have to bring over all our historical transactions?

Usually not. Most companies bring over open items, balances and a summary of past results, and keep the old system or an export for reference. Detailed history can be migrated, but it adds cost and rarely pays off.

Who cleans up the data?

We do the heavy lifting: finding duplicates, gaps and inconsistent codes, and fixing what can be fixed automatically. Your team makes the business decisions, such as which customers are inactive and which item numbers to keep.

Can we keep using our old system during the project?

Yes. You keep working as normal until cutover. We take a final extract at an agreed point, load it, reconcile it and then you switch to Business Central.
