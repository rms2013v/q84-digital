Dynamics GP end of support: dates and your options

Microsoft is retiring Dynamics GP. Here’s exactly what ends and when, what keeps working, and how to plan a move on your own timetable.

DatesWhat stopsOptionsTimelineCostFAQ
Book a free ERP assessment

The short answer: Microsoft ends product support for Dynamics GP on December 31, 2029. That’s the last day for product updates, tax and regulatory updates and technical support. Security patches, if needed, continue until April 30, 2031. Older GP versions lose support sooner: GP 2018 on January 11, 2028, and GP 2016 already lost support on July 14, 2026. Microsoft has also stopped selling GP to new customers and points existing customers to Dynamics 365 Business Central.

GP won’t switch off on those dates. But every month you run it past them, you carry more risk: tax changes you have to handle yourself, no help when something breaks, and fewer people who know the product.

What stops

What stops, and what keeps working

Support ending is a gradual risk, not a cliff. These are the parts of GP that feel it first.

  • Tax and year-end updates stop. Changes to payroll tax tables, 1099 and T4 forms and sales tax rules are no longer delivered after 2029.
  • No technical support. If an update to Windows, SQL Server or an add-on breaks GP, Microsoft won’t help fix it.
  • Add-on vendors move on. Many ISVs that extend GP are already moving their products and people to Business Central.
  • Fewer GP specialists. Consultants who know GP well are retiring or retraining, and their rates rise as they get scarce.
  • Security patches end in 2031. After April 30, 2031, newly found vulnerabilities in GP stay open.
  • Your data stays yours. GP keeps running and your history stays readable for as long as you keep the system.
Your options

Three ways to respond

There’s no single right answer, but there is a wrong one: waiting until 2029 and choosing in a hurry.

Option 1

Stay on GP for now

Reasonable for a year or two if GP still fits and you have a plan. Keep GP current, keep your enhancement plan, and set a decision date well before the end of 2029.

Option 2 · Microsoft’s path

Move to Business Central

The closest fit for most GP users. Microsoft’s migration tools bring master data, open transactions and history across, and the finance concepts will feel familiar. See our GP migration approach.

Option 3

Choose another ERP

Worth a look if your business has changed a lot. Compare honestly: NetSuite, Sage Intacct and Odoo all mean a fresh migration without GP-specific tools.

Plan backwards

A calm timeline to leave GP

Work back from the date you want to be live, not from Microsoft’s deadline. Most companies aim to go live at the start of a fiscal year, with payroll moving on January 1.

  • Now: take stock. List your GP modules, add-ons, customizations, reports, integrations and who uses what. Our ERP readiness checklist helps.
  • 12 to 18 months before go-live: choose. Decide on Business Central or another ERP, and get a written scope and quote.
  • 6 to 9 months before: build. Design, set up, migrate data into a test company and replace add-ons.
  • 2 to 3 months before: test. Your team runs real processes and signs off balances against GP.
  • Go-live: cut over. Final data load at a period end, with support for the first month-end close. Keep GP read-only for lookups.

FAQ

Dynamics GP end of support questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

Will Dynamics GP stop working after December 31, 2029?

No. GP keeps running after support ends. What stops is Microsoft’s help: product updates, tax and regulatory updates and technical support. Security patches, if needed, continue until April 30, 2031. After that, you run GP with no fixes of any kind.

Can we still buy Dynamics GP licenses?

Microsoft has stopped selling Dynamics GP to new customers. Existing customers can keep using and paying for their current GP licenses and enhancement plan until support ends.

What happens to GP payroll?

Payroll is often the first thing to hurt, because payroll tax updates stop with product support. Business Central doesn’t include payroll, so GP payroll users move payroll to a payroll provider that connects to Business Central. Plan this early: payroll changes are easiest at the start of a calendar year.

Is Business Central the only option?

No, but it’s the one Microsoft built a migration path for. Business Central includes tools that bring GP master data, open transactions and history across. Other ERPs such as NetSuite or Sage Intacct mean a fresh data migration without those tools. Business Central vs NetSuite →

When should we start?

Most GP moves take three to six months once the project starts, and you’ll want a buffer for testing, year-end and payroll timing. Starting the assessment one to two years before the support end date lets you pick a calm go-live date instead of a rushed one. Implementation timeline guide →
