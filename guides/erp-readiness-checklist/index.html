In depth

How to run an ERP readiness review with your team

The checklist works best as a team exercise rather than something one person fills in alone. A simple way to run it:

  1. Assign sections. Give each area of the checklist to the person who runs it: finance, sales, purchasing, warehouse, IT.
  2. Answer honestly. Mark each question Yes, Partly or No. “Partly” is the most useful answer; it shows where decisions are still open.
  3. Meet for an hour. Go through the No and Partly answers together and agree an owner for each.
  4. Pick your top five. Write down the five questions that matter most to your business and bring them to your first conversation with any ERP partner.

Teams that do this before talking to vendors get better proposals, because partners can quote against real requirements instead of guesses. It also surfaces disagreements inside the business early, when they’re cheap to resolve.

Who should fill in each section

Finance and reporting goes to your controller or bookkeeper. Inventory, purchasing and sales go to the people who run purchasing and order entry. Warehouse and fulfillment goes to your warehouse lead. Data, integrations and technology goes to whoever manages IT or your current systems. The owner or general manager should answer the goals, budget and timeline sections, because those are the decisions that shape everything else.

Once the checklist is done, keep it. It becomes a useful reference during discovery workshops and a reminder of what “done” should look like at go-live.
