How to use these guides

These guides are written for owners, CFOs and operations leaders at small and midsize companies who are considering Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, or already use it and want to get more from it. Each one answers a question we hear in almost every first conversation.

  • Just starting to look? Begin with the ERP readiness checklist to get your team aligned, then read what Business Central does.
  • Comparing systems? Our comparisons with NetSuite, Sage Intacct and Odoo explain where each one fits better.
  • Building a budget? The cost guide, timeline guide and license calculator give you realistic numbers to plan with.
  • Replacing an older system? See our migration guides for QuickBooks, Dynamics GP, Dynamics NAV, Sage and spreadsheets.

All guides are free and need no email address. They’re written by our senior consultants and updated when Microsoft’s products or prices change.
