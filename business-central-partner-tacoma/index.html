In depth

Why work with a local Business Central consultant

Most of a Business Central project can be delivered remotely, and much of ours is. But a few moments are worth being in the same room for: the first discovery workshops, walking the warehouse floor to see how goods actually move, training sessions, and go-live day. A consultant based in Tacoma can be there for those without travel costs on your invoice.

Puget Sound businesses we know well

The region’s economy leans heavily on trade, logistics and manufacturing. Importers bringing goods through the Port of Tacoma and the Port of Seattle need landed costs on every container. Warehouses in the Kent Valley and Fife need accurate bins and fast picking. Manufacturers across Pierce and King counties need production planning and real product costs. Professional services firms in Seattle and Bellevue need project accounting and time tracking. Business Central handles all of these, and we tailor the setup to which one you are.

Washington-specific setup

Washington uses destination-based sales tax, so the rate depends on where goods are delivered. Business Central handles this with tax areas and jurisdictions, and companies shipping to many locations often connect a tax engine. Washington’s business and occupation (B&O) tax is calculated on gross receipts, so we set up your chart of accounts and dimensions to make B&O reporting by classification straightforward.

FAQ

Questions from Washington businesses

Have a different question? Ask directly.

Do you meet clients in person?

Yes, for companies in the Seattle–Tacoma area. Discovery workshops, warehouse walkthroughs, training and go-live support can happen at your office or warehouse. Day-to-day project work happens online.

Can Business Central handle Washington sales tax?

Yes. Washington uses destination-based sales tax, so the rate depends on where goods are delivered. Business Central handles this with tax areas and jurisdictions, or you can connect a tax engine such as Avalara if you ship to many locations or several states.

Do you only work with companies in Washington?

No. We’re based in Tacoma, but Business Central is a cloud system and we work remotely with companies across the US.
