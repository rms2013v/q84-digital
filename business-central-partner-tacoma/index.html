Business Central in Washington

Set up for how Washington companies work

A few Washington rules affect how Business Central should be set up. Getting them right early saves rework after go-live.

  • Destination-based sales tax. Tax areas and jurisdictions so each delivery gets the right local rate, or a tax engine if you ship to many locations.
  • B&O tax. Washington’s business and occupation tax is based on gross receipts, so we set up accounts and dimensions that make reporting by classification straightforward.
  • Selling into Canada. USD and CAD customers, vendors and bank accounts, or a US and a Canadian company side by side with consolidated reporting.

Why choose a Business Central partner based in Tacoma?

Most of a Business Central project can happen online, but discovery workshops, warehouse walkthroughs, training and go-live day go better in person. With an office in Tacoma, we can be there for those moments without travel costs on your invoice, and we work the same Pacific hours as your team.

Do you meet clients in person?

Yes, for companies in the Seattle–Tacoma area. Discovery workshops, warehouse walkthroughs, training and go-live support can happen at your office or warehouse. Day-to-day project work happens online.

Can Business Central handle Washington sales tax?

Yes. Washington uses destination-based sales tax, so the rate depends on where goods are delivered. Business Central handles this with tax areas and jurisdictions, or you can connect a tax engine such as Avalara if you ship to many locations or several states.

How much does a Business Central implementation cost?

It depends on the number of users, the processes you need, how much data you bring over and which integrations you need. After a free assessment and a short discovery, we give you a written, fixed scope so you know the cost before the project starts. Business Central implementation cost guide →
