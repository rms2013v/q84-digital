What is Business Central?

One ERP system. Your entire business.

FAQ

Common questions

Don't see your question? Ask us directly.

What is Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central?

It's Microsoft's cloud ERP for small and midsize businesses. It brings accounting, inventory, sales, purchasing, warehousing and reporting into one system that works with Microsoft 365.

Is Business Central suitable for small businesses?

Yes, especially once you have inventory, multiple users or more complex reporting than entry-level accounting software handles well.

How much does Business Central cost?

Licensing is a monthly per-user subscription from Microsoft. Implementation is a one-time project cost based on scope. See the pricing breakdown.

How long does implementation take?

A focused finance and inventory rollout often takes a few months. Projects with manufacturing, warehousing or several integrations take longer. We give you a timeline after discovery.

Can Business Central replace QuickBooks?

Yes. Many companies move from QuickBooks when they need stronger inventory, multi-entity accounting or better reporting. We handle the data migration.

Can I migrate data from my existing ERP?

Yes. We map, clean, load and reconcile data from QuickBooks, Sage, Dynamics NAV, spreadsheets and other systems.

Does Business Central support warehouse management?

Yes, including receipts, put-away, picking, shipping and bin locations. The right setup depends on how complex your warehouse is.

Can Business Central integrate with Shopify?

Yes. Business Central has a Shopify connector, and we can build other integrations through its APIs. See integrations.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes, hourly or through a monthly plan. See support options.

Common problems

Why ERP projects go sideways

  • Vague scope. Nobody agrees on what "done" means, so the budget keeps moving.
  • Dirty data. Old records get loaded as-is and cause problems from day one.
  • Too much customization. Custom code replaces standard features and makes upgrades painful.
  • Rushed testing. Real processes aren't tested end to end before go-live.
  • Untrained users. People go back to spreadsheets because nobody showed them how.

What drives the size of a project

We give you a fixed scope and estimate after discovery, so the number is based on your business and not a guess.

  • Modules in scope. Finance only, or also inventory, warehousing and manufacturing.
  • Number of companies and locations. Multi-entity and multi-warehouse setups take more work.

  • Integrations. Each connected system adds design and testing time.
  • Custom development. Extensions are scoped and estimated separately.

Licence types

Choosing the right licences

Microsoft sets Business Central licence prices. Most companies combine a few full users with lower-cost licences for people who only view data or do light tasks.

LicenceWho it's forTypical use
EssentialsFull usersFinance, sales, purchasing, inventory, warehousing, projects
PremiumFull users who need moreEverything in Essentials, plus manufacturing and service management
Team MembersLight usersView data, approve documents, update basic records

Current per-user prices are published by Microsoft and change from time to time. We'll confirm today's pricing in your estimate. Microsoft licence fees are separate from our consulting fees.

Our approach

Configure first, customize only when it pays off

Every custom line of code is something to maintain. We check whether standard features or an existing app can do the job before we build.

  • Fit check. Standard setup, AppSource app or custom extension: we compare the options with you.
  • Upgrade-safe. Extensions follow Microsoft's model, so updates don't break your system.
  • Documented. You get notes on what was built and how it works.
  • Tested. Integrations are tested with real data volumes and error cases.
