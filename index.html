What your team will work in every day

Business Central puts finance, stock, orders and the warehouse on the same screens. Here's what each area looks like.

Financial and operational visibility

Each person opens Business Central to a home page built for their role, with the numbers they need first.

  • Cash, receivables and sales at a glance
  • Drill into any figure to see the entries behind it
  • Power BI reports on live data

Stock, locations and item tracking

See what you have, where it is and what it cost, across every warehouse and location.

  • Stock by location and bin
  • Reorder points and planning suggestions
  • Lot and serial number tracking

Customer orders and invoicing

Go from quote to order to shipment to invoice without retyping anything.

  • Prices, discounts and credit limits applied automatically
  • Stock checked as you enter lines
  • Post the invoice and the ledger updates

Picking, shipping and inventory movements

Warehouse staff get clear take-and-place instructions by bin, on a desktop or a handheld device.

  • Receive, put away, pick and ship
  • Bin-level stock that stays accurate
  • Shipments post straight to the sales order

FAQ

Common questions

Don’t see yours? Ask directly.

What is Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central?

It’s Microsoft’s cloud ERP for small and midsize businesses. It brings accounting, inventory, sales, purchasing, warehousing and reporting into one system that works with Microsoft 365.

Is Business Central right for a distribution or inventory-heavy business?

For most small and midsize distributors, yes. It handles multi-location stock, purchasing, bins, picking and shipping. If your warehouse is very high-volume or complex, we’ll tell you whether an extension or Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management fits better. Is Business Central enough? →

How much does Business Central cost?

Licenses are a monthly per-user subscription from Microsoft. Implementation is a one-time project quoted after a free discovery call. Our calculator estimates your license cost in a minute. Estimate your costs →

How long does implementation take?

A focused finance and inventory rollout usually takes a few months. Projects with manufacturing, advanced warehousing or several integrations take longer. You get a timeline after discovery. How implementation works →

Can Business Central replace QuickBooks?

Yes. Companies usually move when they need proper inventory, multiple companies or better reporting. We handle the data migration. Data migration →

Can Business Central integrate with Shopify?

Yes. Business Central has a Shopify connector, and we build other integrations through its APIs. Integrations →

Are you a Microsoft partner?

Yes. Q84 Digital is a Microsoft partner with full access to Microsoft partner benefits. We help you choose the right licenses, set up Business Central and keep it right-sized as you grow. Licensing & pricing →
