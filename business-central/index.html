Core features

One system for the whole business

Financial management

General ledger, payables, receivables, bank reconciliation, dimensions and financial reporting.

Inventory management

Items, variants, locations, costing methods, reorder points and planning suggestions.

Sales

Quotes, orders, invoicing, pricing, discounts, credit limits and customer management.

Purchasing

Purchase orders, vendor management, receiving, approvals and drop shipments.

Warehouse operations

Receipts, put-away, picking, shipping, bins and inventory movements.

Manufacturing (Premium)

Bills of materials, routings, production orders and capacity planning.

Reporting & analytics

Role-based home pages, Excel analysis and Power BI on live data.

Integrations

Extensions

Apps from Microsoft AppSource and custom AL extensions for specific needs.

Choosing the right ERP

Is Business Central enough for your operations?

For most small and midsize distributors it is. For very high-volume or complex warehousing, an extension or Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management may be a better fit. Because we've worked hands-on with both, we can give you a straight answer.

Often the right fit

Business Central

Standard inventory and warehouse processes: receiving, put-away, picking, shipping and bin-level stock for small and midsize operations.

When you need more

Business Central plus a warehouse extension

Barcode scanning, handheld devices or more advanced picking, added through a specialist app from Microsoft AppSource.

For complex operations

Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management

High-volume or multi-site warehousing, advanced wave picking, transportation management and complex supply chains.

Moving from QuickBooks?

Signs you've outgrown entry-level accounting

  • Inventory lives in spreadsheets. Stock counts and costs are tracked outside your accounting system.
  • Several companies or locations. Consolidation and inter-company work take days.
  • Reporting is manual. Month-end means exporting and rebuilding reports in Excel.
  • Too many disconnected tools. Orders, stock and accounting are reconciled by hand.
