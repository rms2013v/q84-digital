Why companies move

Signs you’ve outgrown QuickBooks

QuickBooks is a good accounting system for a small company. It wasn’t built to run inventory, warehouses and several entities. For a closer look, read seven signs your distribution business has outgrown QuickBooks.

  • Inventory lives in spreadsheets. Stock and costs are tracked outside your books. We move those too.
  • More than one location or company. Consolidation and inter-company work take days.
  • Month-end takes a week. Reports are rebuilt in Excel every month.
  • File size and speed. QuickBooks Desktop slows down or hits its limits.
  • No real controls. Approvals, user permissions and audit trails are thin.

Worked example · Illustrative scenario

Moving a 25-person distributor off QuickBooks Desktop

A distributor runs accounting in QuickBooks Desktop with around 1,800 customers. Its 4,000 items and stock levels for two warehouses live in spreadsheets, and month-end takes a week of reconciling.

StageWhat happens
Weeks 1 to 2Map QuickBooks customers, vendors and accounts to Business Central. Merge the item spreadsheets into one list and agree cleansing rules with the owner.
Week 3First trial load into a test company. Duplicate customers and inconsistent unit-of-measure codes are found and fixed.
Week 4Finance posts test invoices and payments. The warehouse lead checks stock by location and bin.
Week 5Second trial load. Receivables, payables and the trial balance reconcile to QuickBooks.
Cutover weekendPhysical stock count, final load of open items and opening balances, and sign-off before Monday trading.

Outcome: The company starts in Business Central with balances that match QuickBooks to the cent, one item list and stock it can trust, with no gap in trading.

In depth

Planning a QuickBooks to Business Central move

QuickBooks Online vs QuickBooks Desktop

The destination is the same, but the starting point differs. QuickBooks Online data is extracted through exports and its reports; QuickBooks Desktop data comes from exports and the company file’s reports, and older Desktop files often carry more years of inactive records. Either way, we work from reports you can reconcile against: the trial balance, A/R and A/P aging, and an inventory valuation if you track stock in QuickBooks.

Things QuickBooks users notice first in Business Central

  • Posting groups. Instead of choosing an account on every transaction, customers, vendors and items carry posting groups that decide the accounts automatically. This is what keeps the books consistent as volume grows.
  • Documents, then posting. Orders and invoices are created, reviewed and then posted. Posted entries can’t be edited, only reversed, which gives you a real audit trail.
  • Dimensions instead of classes. Department, location or channel tags work across every report and Power BI.
  • Real inventory. Stock by location and bin, reservations against orders, and costs that flow to the general ledger.

A realistic timeline

For a small distributor, the QuickBooks data migration typically runs over four to six weeks alongside the wider implementation, with two trial loads before the final cutover. Most companies go live at the start of a month so the opening balances line up with a clean period end. Keep QuickBooks available in read-only mode, or keep an export, so you can look up historical transactions after go-live.

What to do in QuickBooks before you start

  • Make customers, vendors and items you no longer use inactive.
  • Reconcile bank accounts and clear old uncleared items.
  • Void or close invoices and bills that will never be paid.
  • Agree a cutover date with your accountant, ideally a month or quarter end.
FAQ

QuickBooks migration questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

Can we migrate from both QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop?

Yes. The data we bring over is the same: chart of accounts, customers, vendors, items, open invoices and bills, and balances. Only the way we extract it differs.

What happens to payroll?

Business Central doesn’t include US payroll. Most companies keep a payroll provider such as ADP or Gusto and post payroll to Business Central as a journal, or connect it with an app.

Do QuickBooks classes and locations carry over?

Yes, as dimensions in Business Central. Dimensions do the same job with more flexibility, so you can report profit by department, location or project.

Should we bring our transaction history?

Usually just open items, balances and monthly totals for one or two prior years. Keep QuickBooks or an export for looking up old invoices. Choosing how much history →
