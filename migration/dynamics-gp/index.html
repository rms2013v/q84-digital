Dynamics GP to Business Central migration

Microsoft is retiring Dynamics GP. Business Central is its natural successor, and Microsoft provides tools to move your data. We plan the move and handle what the tools don’t.

Why now

Why GP customers are moving

Microsoft has stopped selling Dynamics GP to new customers and has announced an end date for product support. Planning a move now means you choose the timing instead of the deadline choosing it for you.

  • Support has an end date. Tax updates and technical support stop on December 31, 2029, and security patches stop after April 30, 2031.
  • Fewer GP specialists. Finding consultants and add-on vendors gets harder each year.
  • Cloud and Microsoft 365. Business Central runs in the cloud and works with Outlook, Excel and Teams.
  • Microsoft’s migration path. Business Central includes tools built specifically to bring GP data across.

Our process

A GP migration in five steps

  • Inventory your GP. Modules, add-ons, customizations, reports and integrations in use today.
  • Design Business Central. Chart of accounts, dimensions, posting groups and processes set up for how you work now.
  • Run Microsoft’s migration. Master data, open transactions and history brought across into a test company.
  • Test and reconcile. Your team runs real processes and signs off balances against GP.
  • Cut over. Final migration at a period end, with hands-on support for the first close.
In depth

Planning a Dynamics GP to Business Central migration

Use Microsoft’s migration tools, but don’t stop there

Business Central includes a cloud migration path built specifically for Dynamics GP. It brings over master data, open transactions and summary account history, and can add a read-only snapshot of detailed history. What it doesn’t do is redesign anything: GP’s account structure, item classes and posting setup come across largely as they were. A GP migration is the right moment to simplify, so we design the Business Central setup first and then map GP data into it.

Account segments and dimensions

GP stores department, location or division in account segments, which often leads to thousands of accounts. In Business Central, those segments usually become dimensions on a much shorter chart of accounts. The migration tools support this mapping, but the decision about which segments to keep as accounts and which become dimensions is a business decision to make early.

Reports and add-ons

Long-running GP systems typically carry dozens of SmartLists, Management Reporter reports and third-party add-ons. We list them all, find out which are still used, and replace only those: with standard Business Central lists and financial reports, Excel and Power BI, or an AppSource app. Many turn out to be unused.

Timing your move

Because Microsoft has announced an end date for GP support, many GP customers will move over the next few years, and experienced consultants will get busier. Starting the assessment early lets you pick a cutover at a fiscal year or quarter end that suits your business, rather than one forced by the support deadline.

What GP users notice first in Business Central

Business Central runs in a browser instead of a desktop client, uses posting groups to decide accounts automatically, and replaces GP batches with documents and journals that post directly. Finance teams usually adapt quickly; the bigger change is often for operations users, who get more inventory and warehouse capability than GP offered.

FAQ

Dynamics GP migration questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

When does Microsoft stop supporting Dynamics GP?

Microsoft has stopped selling Dynamics GP to new customers and has announced that product support, including tax and regulatory updates and technical support, ends on December 31, 2029. Security patches, if needed, continue until April 30, 2031. Check Microsoft’s Dynamics GP lifecycle page for the latest dates.

Can we bring our GP history?

Yes. Microsoft’s migration brings summary account history and can add a read-only snapshot of detailed historical transactions, so you can look up old GP documents inside Business Central.

What happens to our GP add-ons?

Each third-party GP product needs a decision: a standard Business Central feature, an AppSource app, or a small extension. We list them all during discovery so nothing is missed.

Is this an upgrade or a new implementation?

It’s a new implementation with a guided data migration. Business Central works differently from GP, so we set it up around how you work today rather than copying GP’s structure.
