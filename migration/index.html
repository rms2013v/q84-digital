In depth

Common data problems we find, and how we fix them

Almost every migration turns up the same kinds of data issues. Finding them in a trial load, rather than on go-live day, is the whole point of the process.

  • Duplicate customers and vendors created under slightly different names over the years. We match on tax IDs, addresses and emails, and you decide which record survives.
  • Inconsistent units of measure, such as “EA”, “each” and “pcs” for the same thing. We standardize them before items load.
  • Items without costs or with negative stock. These distort inventory value and must be corrected before opening balances are posted.
  • Open documents that are really closed: invoices paid outside the system, or orders that will never ship.
  • Balances that don’t tie out between subledgers and the general ledger. We reconcile these in the old system first, so you don’t carry the gap into Business Central.

Cutover weekend

Final cutover usually happens over a weekend at a month or quarter end. The old system is closed for new transactions on Friday, final balances and open documents are extracted, a physical stock count is taken, and everything is loaded and reconciled in Business Central. Your finance lead signs off the numbers before trading starts on Monday.

Every migration ends with a signed reconciliation, so you know exactly which numbers came across and that they match.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

How long does a data migration take?

For most small and midsize businesses the migration runs alongside the implementation over three to six weeks, with at least two trial loads before the final cutover. The final load itself usually happens over a weekend or at a month-end.

Do we have to bring over all our historical transactions?

Usually not. Most companies bring over open items, balances and a summary of past results, and keep the old system or an export for reference. Detailed history can be migrated, but it adds cost and rarely pays off.

Who cleans up the data?

We do the heavy lifting: finding duplicates, gaps and inconsistent codes, and fixing what can be fixed automatically. Your team makes the business decisions, such as which customers are inactive and which item numbers to keep.

Can we keep using our old system during the project?

Yes. You keep working as normal until cutover. We take a final extract at an agreed point, load it, reconcile it and then you switch to Business Central.
