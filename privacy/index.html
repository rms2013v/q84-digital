Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026.

This policy explains how Q84 Digital handles personal information you share through this website.

What we collect

When you request a consultation we collect the details you enter: your name, business email, company, the topic you choose and any message you write.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your request, arrange a consultation and follow up about your project. We don't sell or rent it, and we don't add you to marketing lists without your consent.

Analytics

This site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Embedded videos

Some pages include videos hosted on YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. When you do, the video plays from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and YouTube’s privacy policy applies.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run this website and handle email, and only as needed to provide those services, or where the law requires it.

Retention and your rights

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting work. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@q84digital.com. Under Canada's PIPEDA you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
