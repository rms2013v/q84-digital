Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026.

This policy explains how Q84 Digital handles personal information you share through this website.

What we collect

When you request a consultation we collect the details you enter: your name, business email, company, the topic you choose and any message you write. When you download the ERP Readiness Checklist we collect your email address and, if you enter them, your first name and company.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your request, arrange a consultation and follow up about your project. We don't sell or rent it, and we don't add you to marketing lists without your consent.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use this site, such as which pages are viewed and when a form is sent. It sets cookies and collects information like your approximate location and device type. It does not tell us who you are. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

We use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use this site, through heatmaps and session recordings of clicks, scrolling and mouse movement. Clarity sets cookies and collects information like your device, browser and approximate location. Text you type into forms is masked and not recorded. The data is processed by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Embedded videos

Some pages include videos hosted on YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. When you do, the video plays from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and YouTube’s privacy policy applies.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run this website and handle email, and only as needed to provide those services, or where the law requires it.

Retention and your rights

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting work. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@q84digital.com. If you have a concern we haven’t resolved, you can also contact the Washington State Attorney General’s Office.
