Our approach

Configure first, customize only when it pays off

Every custom line of code is something to maintain. We check whether standard features or an existing app can do the job before we build anything.

  • Fit check. Standard setup, AppSource app or custom extension: we compare the options with you.
  • Built for updates. Extensions follow Microsoft’s extension model, which keeps Business Central updates much smoother. We still test custom work against each major release.
  • Documented. You get notes on what was built and how it works.
  • Tested. Integrations are tested with realistic data volumes and error cases.
Also running Dynamics 365 Sales, Customer Service or Field Service? We work across the Dynamics 365 family and can connect Business Central with Dataverse and your CRM.
