Connecting a Shopify store to Business Central

An e-commerce distributor sells through Shopify and keeps its accounting in Business Central. Staff re-enter every web order, stock levels are updated by hand twice a day, and the store sometimes sells items that are out of stock.

StageWhat happens
DesignAgree that Business Central owns items, prices and stock, and Shopify owns web orders. Map product variants to item variants.
SetupConfigure Microsoft’s Shopify connector: item sync, stock sync by location, and order import with customer matching.
RulesOrders with unknown items or failed payments go to a review list instead of creating bad sales orders.
TestingRun test orders, refunds and partial shipments through the full flow, from Shopify order to shipment, invoice and payment.
Go-liveSwitch on scheduled sync and train staff to clear the exceptions list each morning.

Outcome: Web orders arrive in Business Central without retyping, the store shows stock that matches the warehouse, and staff only touch the orders that genuinely need attention.

This example shows how we approach this kind of project. It is not a client case study.

Beyond the basics

When you need more than the connector

  • Bundles and kits. Sold as one product, shipped as several items.
  • Marketplaces. Amazon, Walmart and other channels through specialist connector apps.
  • 3PL fulfillment. Orders passed to a third-party warehouse and confirmed back.
  • Custom rules. Order routing, fraud holds or special pricing logic built as small extensions.
In depth

Setting up the Shopify connector well

Decisions to make first

  • Which locations feed Shopify stock. You may not want every warehouse, or stock reserved for wholesale customers, to show as available online.
  • How orders import. Automatically as sales orders, as invoices for prepaid orders, or into a review step first.
  • Customer handling. One generic web customer or a Business Central customer per buyer. Individual customers give better reporting; a generic customer keeps the customer list small.
  • Taxes and shipping charges. How Shopify’s tax and shipping lines map to Business Central accounts.

Payouts and fees

Shopify payouts combine sales, refunds and fees. Importing payout transactions lets finance match each payout to the bank deposit and post fees to their own account, so gross sales and fees are visible separately.

Living with it day to day

Once set up, the connector runs on a schedule. The daily routine is short: check the exceptions list for orders that couldn’t be created, usually because of a new product or an unusual address, and fix them at the source so they don’t repeat.

Multiple stores and markets

Brands with separate stores for different countries, brands or wholesale can connect each store to the same Business Central company, each with its own settings for prices, stock locations and order handling. Stock and sales from every store then roll up into one set of books and one inventory position.

Before go-live we test the full flow with real products: orders, partial shipments, refunds, cancellations and a payout, so the first live weekend holds no surprises.

After go-live, review the connector settings when you add a new store, location, product type or payment method, since each can need a small configuration change.

FAQ

Shopify integration questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

Does the Shopify connector cost extra?

No. Microsoft’s Shopify connector is included with Business Central. You pay for your Shopify plan and Business Central licenses as usual.

Can we connect more than one Shopify store?

Yes. The connector supports several stores, each with its own settings for items, prices, locations and order handling.

Which system should own prices and stock?

Usually Business Central. It knows what’s in every warehouse and what’s already promised to other orders, so it should tell Shopify what’s available to sell.

What about Shopify B2B?

The connector supports Shopify’s B2B features on Shopify Plus, including company accounts and their locations. We check which parts you need during setup.
