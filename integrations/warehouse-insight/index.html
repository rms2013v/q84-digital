Warehouse Insight mobile WMS for Business Central

A scanner-first warehouse app for Business Central. Your team receives, puts away, picks, ships and counts on handheld devices, and every scan updates Business Central straight away.

What it is

Business Central in your warehouse team’s hands

Warehouse Insight is an advanced warehouse management app for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. It runs on rugged handhelds and puts every everyday warehouse task on one simple home screen, with large buttons designed for scanning on the move.

Instead of walking back to a desk to update the system, staff scan the item, the bin and the quantity where the work happens. Business Central stays accurate in real time, and paper pick lists and count sheets disappear.

Functions

Every warehouse task on one screen

Item and bin inquiry

Scan an item to see where it is and how many are available, or scan a bin to see what it holds.

Receive and put-away

Receive against purchase orders by scanning, then put goods away into the right bins.

Pick and ship

Follow picks bin by bin, confirm each item with a scan, and ship from the device.

Movement and ad-hoc moves

Process planned movements, or move stock between bins on the spot when the floor needs it.

Counts

Cycle counts and basic counts by scanning, without printed count sheets.

Consumption, output and assembly

Record materials used and finished goods produced, and process assembly orders for kits.

License plates

Track pallets and cartons as license plates, so a whole pallet moves or ships with one scan.

Scratchpad

Capture quick scans and notes on the device for tasks that don’t fit a standard document.

Live in Business Central

Every scan posts or updates the Business Central document, so stock and orders are always current.

Why it matters

What changes on the warehouse floor

Scanning removes the two biggest sources of warehouse errors: picking the wrong item and typing the wrong quantity.

  • Fewer picking errors. A scan confirms the right item and bin before anything ships.
  • Inventory you can trust. Stock moves are recorded as they happen, not at the end of the day.
  • Faster receiving and shipping. No paperwork to key in afterwards.
  • Counting without shutdowns. Count a few bins every day instead of closing for a full count.
  • Pallet-level tracking. License plates keep pallets and cartons together from receipt to shipment.
Parcel shipping

Ship parcels with Order Ship Express

Once an order is picked and packed, it still needs a carrier label. Order Ship Express is a free shipping app for Business Central from Insight Works, the publisher of Warehouse Insight. It connects Business Central to carriers through EasyPost, so your team can compare rates and print labels without leaving the order.

Live rate shopping

Compare carrier services and prices from the sales order before you choose how to ship.

Multiple carriers

Connect up to three carriers through an EasyPost account, such as UPS, USPS, FedEx or DHL, and pick one per shipment.

Labels from the order

Print carrier-approved labels directly from the sales order, using standard or custom package sizes, with no retyping.

Tracking back in Business Central

Tracking numbers and freight charges are written back to the sales order, so customer service can answer “where is my order?” from Business Central.

Return labels

Create return labels from posted shipments or invoices, something standard Business Central doesn’t offer.

Freight charges and markups

Add shipping charges to orders automatically, with optional markup rules per carrier for what you bill customers.

Order Ship Express has no license fee; you pay the carriers for postage through EasyPost. It is built for domestic parcel shipping in North America. For international shipping, more than three carriers, or LTL and full-truckload freight, Insight Works offers a separate, more advanced shipping app, and we can help you choose.

In depth

Getting your warehouse ready for Warehouse Insight

A mobile warehouse app works best when the basics in Business Central are already right. Before rollout we check four things:

  • Bins that match the floor. Every rack position has a bin code in Business Central and a label on the shelf.
  • Barcodes on items. Supplier barcodes are recorded as item references, and items without one get your own labels.
  • Warehouse documents per location. Receipts, put-aways, picks and shipments are switched on for the locations that will use scanning.
  • Wi-Fi in every aisle. Coverage is tested with the actual devices, including cold rooms, mezzanines and the loading dock.

We then roll out one process at a time, usually receiving or picking first, and add counting, moves and license plates once the team is comfortable.

Label printing

Scanning usually goes hand in hand with label printing: item labels at receiving, bin labels for shelving, and shipping labels at packing. Business Central report layouts can print labels to standard label printers, and warehouse and shipping apps add carrier and compliance labels.

Measuring the result

Track a few numbers before and after rollout: picking errors, inventory accuracy from cycle counts, time from order release to shipment, and how many documents are still entered at a desk. These show whether scanning is paying off and where to focus next.

Training the warehouse team

Warehouse staff learn scanning fastest on the floor, not in a meeting room. Short, hands-on sessions on real orders, a one-page guide for each process kept near the packing benches, and a named person to ask for the first few weeks make adoption much smoother. Expect a short dip in speed during the first week, followed by faster and more accurate work than before.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Buying devices before testing them with the chosen app.
  • Starting scanning before bins and item barcodes are set up and labeled.
  • Rolling out every process at once instead of one at a time.
FAQ

Warehouse Insight questions

Have a different question? Ask directly.

Does Warehouse Insight replace Business Central’s warehouse features?

No. It works on top of them. Receipts, put-aways, picks, shipments and counts are still Business Central documents; Warehouse Insight gives your team a fast, scanner-first way to process them on the floor.

Which devices does it run on?

Rugged Android handhelds with built-in barcode scanners are the usual choice for warehouse work. We help you choose and test devices before you buy a fleet.

Does it support manufacturing?

Yes. The app includes consumption and output, so shop-floor staff can record materials used and finished goods produced against production orders, and assembly orders for kitting. Business Central for manufacturing →

How is it licensed?

Warehouse Insight is an add-on app available through Microsoft AppSource, with its own subscription from the publisher, separate from your Business Central licenses. We include the cost in your estimate.

What does Order Ship Express cost?

The app itself is free. You pay the carriers for postage through an EasyPost account, and EasyPost’s discounted rates can lower what you pay today. You need accounts with the carriers you want to use.

Can Order Ship Express handle international or freight shipments?

It is designed for domestic parcel shipping with up to three carriers. For international parcels, more carriers or LTL and truckload freight, a more advanced shipping app is the better fit, and we’ll recommend one during discovery.
