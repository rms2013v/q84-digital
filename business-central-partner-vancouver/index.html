Business Central in Canada

Set up for how Canadian companies work

Business Central has a Canadian version, and getting its Canadian features right early saves a lot of rework after go-live.

  • GST, PST and HST. Tax areas and groups for BC sales, other provinces and US states, with the reports your accountant needs.
  • CAD and USD. Multi-currency customers, vendors and bank accounts, with exchange rate gains and losses posted for you.
  • Cross-border companies. A Canadian and a US company side by side, with intercompany transactions and consolidated reporting.
  • Canadian banks. Bank feeds, reconciliation and electronic payments to vendors.

FAQ

Questions from BC businesses

Have a different question? Ask directly.

Do you meet clients in person in Vancouver?

Yes. Our office is at 555 Burrard St in downtown Vancouver, and we work on site with companies across the Vancouver area for discovery workshops, warehouse walkthroughs, training and go-live support. Day-to-day project work happens online.

Can Business Central handle GST and PST in British Columbia?

Yes. The Canadian version of Business Central handles GST, PST and HST with tax areas and tax groups, so a BC sale can carry 5% GST and 7% PST while a sale shipped to Ontario carries 13% HST. We set up the tax areas, exemptions and reports for the provinces and US states you sell into.

Can you set up a company that trades in both Canada and the US?

Yes. Business Central handles CAD and USD with exchange rates and realized and unrealized gains, and can run a Canadian and a US company side by side with intercompany transactions and consolidated reporting. We have offices in both Vancouver, BC and Tacoma, WA.

Do you only work with companies in British Columbia?

No. We have offices in Vancouver and Tacoma, and Business Central is a cloud system, so we work remotely with companies across Canada and the US.
