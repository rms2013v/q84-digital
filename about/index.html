Who we are

A senior team on every project

Q84 Digital is a team of senior Microsoft Dynamics consultants. Every consultant has more than 10 years of hands-on ERP experience in finance, supply chain and operations, across the Dynamics 365 family, from Business Central to Supply Chain Management, Customer Engagement and Field Service.

That range matters when you choose an ERP. Because our team has worked hands-on with both Business Central and the larger Dynamics 365 products, we can tell you honestly when Business Central is enough and when it isn’t.

We keep the team senior on purpose. The consultants who run your discovery workshops are the ones who configure the system, train your people and stand next to you at go-live.

How we work

In depth

Why Q84 Digital exists

Many small and midsize companies end up with an ERP partner that is either too big to give them senior attention or too small to know supply chain well. Q84 Digital was set up to fill that gap: a senior consultant with deep Dynamics 365 and supply chain experience who stays with each client from the first conversation to everyday support.

We keep the client list deliberately small so every project gets that attention. If a project needs more hands, we bring in trusted specialists for specific pieces such as EDI or custom development, and we stay accountable for the result.
