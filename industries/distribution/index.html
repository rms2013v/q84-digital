Common challenges

Where distributors lose time and margin

  • Inventory you can’t trust. System counts drift from what’s on the shelf.
  • Manual purchasing. Buyers reorder from memory or spreadsheets.
  • Picking errors. Wrong items or quantities shipped, and costly returns.
  • Hidden costs. Freight and duty aren’t added to item costs, so margins look better than they are.
How Business Central helps

Built around the order-to-cash and procure-to-pay flow

Multi-location inventory

Stock by location and bin, transfers, cycle counts and item tracking.

Purchasing & replenishment

Reorder points, planning suggestions and vendor lead times turned into purchase orders.

Warehouse processes

Receiving, put-away, picking and shipping documents by bin.

Shipping

Shipment documents, carrier integrations and tracking.

Landed cost

Freight, duty and handling charged to the items they belong to.

Margin reporting

Profit by item, customer and salesperson on live data.
