Common challenges

What holds growing manufacturers back

  • No production visibility. Order status lives on the shop floor, not in the system.
  • Costs worked out in spreadsheets. Material and labour costs are estimates.
  • Material shortages. Components run out because demand isn’t planned.
  • Disconnected systems. Production, stock and accounting don’t share data.
