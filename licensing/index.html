    License types

    Business Central Essentials vs Premium

    Essentials covers finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, warehousing and projects, which is enough for most distributors and service businesses. Premium adds manufacturing and service management. Most companies combine a few full users with lower-cost Team Members licenses for people who only view data or do light tasks. All full users in a company must be on the same plan.

    In depth

    How to choose the right Business Central licenses

    Licensing mistakes are usually small but recurring: paying for full users who only look at data, or choosing Essentials and discovering later that you need manufacturing. A short review before you buy avoids both.

    Full users or Team Members?

    Anyone who creates and posts transactions, such as sales orders, purchase orders, invoices, journals or warehouse documents, needs a full user license. People who mostly look things up, approve documents, update simple records or fill in time sheets can usually use a Team Members license at a fraction of the price. In a typical distributor, roughly half the people who touch the system qualify as Team Members.

    When you need Premium

    • Manufacturing: production orders, routings, work and machine centers, and capacity planning.
    • Service management: service orders, service items, contracts and dispatching.

    Assembly orders for simple kitting are available in Essentials. All full users in the same environment must be on the same plan, so if one person needs Premium features, every full user moves to Premium.

    Other costs to plan for

    • Additional environments and storage if you need more sandboxes or a large database.
    • AppSource apps for EDI, shipping, scanning or sales tax, priced monthly by their publishers.
    • Power BI Pro licenses for people who build or share reports, if your Microsoft 365 plan doesn’t include them.

    Monthly or annual commitment

    Microsoft’s list prices assume an annual commitment billed monthly. Your licensing partner can explain the commitment terms and how adding or removing users works during the year. Plan to review your license mix after the first few months live, once you know who actually uses what.
