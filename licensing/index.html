    Implementation scope

    What an implementation includes

    Every project gets a written, fixed scope after discovery. This is what a standard implementation covers, and what is quoted separately.

    Included

    In a standard implementation

    • Discovery workshops and a written solution design
    • Setup of finance, sales, purchasing and inventory
    • Warehouse setup for locations and bins in scope
    • Migration of master data, open items and opening balances
    • Testing with your team and role-based training
    • Go-live support and the first month-end close
    Quoted separately

    Not included by default

    • Microsoft licenses and paid AppSource apps
    • Custom AL extensions and reports
    • Integrations with other systems
    • Migration of detailed transaction history
    • Ongoing support after the go-live period

    Typical timelines

    Typical ranges for small and midsize businesses. Your actual timeline is set after discovery.

    Project sizeTypical scopeTypical timeline

    License types

    Choosing the right licenses

    Most companies combine a few full users with lower-cost Team Members licenses for people who only view data or do light tasks. All full users in a company must be on the same plan.

    LicensePrice (USD)Who it's forTypical use
    Essentials$80 / user / monthFull usersFinance, sales, purchasing, inventory, warehousing, projects
    Premium$110 / user / monthFull users who need moreEverything in Essentials, plus manufacturing and service management
    Team Members$8 / user / monthLight usersView data, approve documents, update basic records

    Prices are Microsoft's US list prices in US dollars, effective November 1, 2025, and may change. We confirm current pricing in every estimate. Microsoft license fees are separate from our consulting fees.
